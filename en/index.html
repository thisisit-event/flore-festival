Our inclusive revolution

A festival where everyone can finally bloom

FLORE is the first gluten-free and lactose-free festival: concerts and the Jardin des Saveurs, a food court and bar where you can order anything with your eyes closed. A real day of celebration, for those who never had the choice and for everyone else.

Gluten-free

The Jardin des Saveurs and the bars, gluten-free and checked stall by stall.

Lactose-free

The Jardin des Saveurs and the bars, entirely lactose-free, so you can enjoy without a second thought.

Music & food court

Live concerts, food trucks and a bar. The full festival vibe, no compromise.

Health & kindness

Health talks, experts, and a safe, caring setting where everyone feels at ease.

FAQ

Your questions, answered

What exactly is FLORE Festival?

FLORE is a one-day music and food festival, entirely gluten-free and lactose-free: concerts, a food court, a shop and wellbeing spaces, all on one site, with every food stall checked before opening.

Is it only for people with allergies or intolerances?

Not at all. FLORE is a festival for everyone, that happens to be gluten-free and lactose-free. Think of it as any other festival: line-up, food, atmosphere, just without the usual dietary stress for those who need it.

When and where will it take place?

Saturday 17 April 2027, at Ainterexpo in Bourg-en-Bresse, France, between Lyon and Geneva.

Are tickets on sale yet?

Presales are sold out. General sale opens on Saturday 5 December at 10am (Paris time), with a Day Pass and an Evening Pass (6pm to 10pm). Join the waiting list using the Get my ticket button to be notified first.

Does the ticket include food and drinks?

No: like at any festival, concert or theme park, your ticket gives you access to the site (stage, shop, talks), but the Jardin des Saveurs food court and the bars are paid for separately, on site. That's exactly what keeps the ticket price so accessible, and we work with every vendor to keep prices fair so you can enjoy several stands without breaking the bank.

Is the food really safe for coeliacs?

That's the whole idea. Our food court (Jardin des Saveurs) and bars follow a strict below-20 ppm gluten standard, the threshold recognised for coeliac disease, with dedicated preparation and every stall checked before opening to avoid cross-contamination.

Do I need to travel far to get there?

No. Ainterexpo is about 50 minutes from Lyon and 1h15 from Geneva, easily reached by car, train or plane.
