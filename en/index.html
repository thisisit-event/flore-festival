An inclusive festival

A festival where everyone can finally bloom

FLORE is the first 100% gluten-free and lactose-free music and food festival. Concerts, DJ sets, food court, bar and shop: everything we love about a festival, without the usual sacrifices. A full day to dance, taste and enjoy, no second-guessing.

🌾

100% gluten-free

The whole site, food court and bar included, is guaranteed gluten-free (below 20 ppm, the strictest standard). Safe even for coeliacs.

🥛

100% lactose-free

Every dairy product is lactose-free or naturally without lactose, plus plant-based options. Real taste, no lactose.

🎶

Music & food court

Live concerts, DJ sets, food trucks and a bar. The full festival vibe, no compromise.

The promise
FLORE isn't a festival for people on a diet. It's a festival, full stop, that happens to be 100% gluten-free and lactose-free, so no one ever has to choose between having fun and eating with peace of mind.
Plan your visit

Easy to reach, from anywhere

In the heart of the Ain region, between Lyon, Geneva and the Alps, and well connected from Switzerland, Italy and Germany.

🚗

By car

At the Salle Polyvalente de Lagnieu (244 Allée Guy de la Verpillière), about 40 min from Lyon and close to Geneva. Visitor parking on site.

🚆

By train

Ambérieu-en-Bugey station is ~30 min from Lyon by regional train, with a festival shuttle to the site.

✈️

By plane

Lyon-Saint-Exupéry airport is ~45 min away, with international connections across Europe.
