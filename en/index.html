An inclusive festival

A festival where everyone can finally bloom

FLORE is the first 100% gluten-free and lactose-free festival. Concerts, DJ sets, food court, bar and shop: everything we love about a festival, without the usual sacrifices. A full day to dance, taste and enjoy, no second-guessing.

🌾

100% gluten-free

The whole site, food court and bar included, is guaranteed gluten-free (below 20 ppm, the strictest standard). Safe even for coeliacs.

🥛

100% lactose-free

Every dairy product is lactose-free or naturally without lactose, plus plant-based options. Real taste, no lactose.

🎶

Music & food court

FAQ

Your questions, answered

What exactly is FLORE Festival?

FLORE is a one-day music and food festival, entirely gluten-free and lactose-free: concerts, DJ sets, a food court, a shop and wellbeing spaces, all on one site, all safe to eat and drink without a second thought.

Is it only for people with allergies or intolerances?

Not at all. FLORE is a festival for everyone, that happens to be 100% gluten-free and lactose-free. Think of it as any other festival: line-up, food, atmosphere, just without the usual dietary stress for those who need it.

When and where will it take place?

The exact date and venue will be announced soon. Sign up to the priority list below to be the first to know, before it's public.

Are tickets on sale yet?

Not yet. The priority list gives you early access and an early-bird price as soon as the box office opens.

Is the food really safe for coeliacs?

Yes. The whole site, food court and bar included, follows a strict below-20ppm gluten standard, the threshold recognised for coeliac disease, with dedicated preparation to avoid cross-contamination.

Do I need to travel far to get there?

No. The venue is being chosen in a region easily reached from Switzerland, northern Italy, southern Germany and all of France, by car, train or plane.
